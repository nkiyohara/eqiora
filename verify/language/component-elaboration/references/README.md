# References

The numerical oracle is the elementary closed-form DC solution. With a 12 V
ideal source across parallel 2 ohm and 4 ohm resistors, Ohm's law gives 6 A and
3 A in the two positive resistor directions. Kirchhoff balance therefore
requires -9 A at the source positive terminal, the opposite currents at the
negative terminals, zero ground potential, and zero signed current sum at
both junctions.

The structural oracle is Eqiora's existing flat scalar physical contract:
one nominal Domain, seven Ports, four Relations with four continuous
Activations, two N-ary Connections, and fourteen across/through unknowns and
residuals. The companion flat fixture is
[`electrical.parallel-dc-network`](../../../electrical/parallel-dc-network/README.md).
Comparison uses a total semantic-identity bijection. The hierarchical source
has its own deterministic namespace, while its public Parameter terms forward
the three root Model Parameter identities through both component levels;
neither source retains occurrence-local Parameter nodes.

The identity oracle is exact equality between the canonical and semantically
permuted hierarchical fixtures. Source spans and file names are deliberately
excluded from that equality and remain observable only through elaboration
provenance.
