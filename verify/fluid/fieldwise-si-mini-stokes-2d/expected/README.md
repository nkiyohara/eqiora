# Acceptance contract

Acceptance requires one closed vertical slice, not the coexistence of separate
semantic and numerical tests.

- Direct-flat and exact-package roots must pass their ordinary compiler and
  package-neutral canonical Stokes lowerer, then bind each recognized role to
  its own exact Semantic identity. The separate registered RFC 0044 case owns
  byte-level verification-private identity normalization; this execution case
  proves corresponding role-bound algebra and physical evidence.
- The exact velocity and pressure Field identities must bind respectively to
  `(P1+bubble)^2` and P1. The force-potential Field remains coefficient data,
  and exactly one Realization-owned zero-integral constraint targets pressure.
- The connected physical `4 x 2` cell mesh has 16 affine triangles. Its mesh
  artifact, digest, intrinsic dimension, and complete canonical-box boundary
  must validate before numerical finalization.
- Both scale profiles must produce canonical Realization v2 bytes and stable
  domain-separated digests. Decode/re-encode is byte-identical, the imported
  mesh and layout remain linked, and Run-manifest v2 rejects any Model,
  Realization, target, topology, layout, or reduction drift.
- Production assembles the dimensionless operator directly. Exact CSR
  symmetry and an independent coefficientwise `D A D / Theta` comparison
  must agree over the complete reduced velocity, pressure, and gauge blocks.
  A separately recomputed true
  dimensionless residual must satisfy the declared tolerance.
- Direct and package paths must agree in dimensionless algebra and reported
  evidence. Profiles A and B must have distinct Realization identities and
  right-hand sides but identical dimensionless matrices and equivalent
  reconstructed physical fields.
- Physical acceptance requires zero velocity, `p = 0.75 Pa/m * (x - 2 m)`,
  zero pressure integral, zero gauge multiplier, body-force resultant
  `(6 N/m, 0)`, reaction `(-6 N/m, 0)`, and their componentwise balance.

The executable falsifiers must close all identity, type, scale, constraint,
mesh, quadrature, solver-property, artifact-replay, and reconstruction near
misses named in `case.toml`. No rejected path may emit accepted solution
evidence or a Run binding that purports to attest execution.
