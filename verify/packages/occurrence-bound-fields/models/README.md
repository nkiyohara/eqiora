# Models

`nested-fields.eqi` is the local canonical fixture. `FieldLaw` declares two
required Field slots over one two-dimensional volume support. `FieldLawWrapper`
forwards those slots without owning Fields, and `Main` binds them to its two
ordinary continuum Fields.

The fixture keeps its Relations algebraically trivial so every observed node,
edge, expression symbol, identity, and provenance span belongs to hierarchy
elaboration rather than a numerical method. The integration test constructs
the exact package and negative sources beside their typed assertions.
