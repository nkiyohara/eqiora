# Thin cylindrical-shell collapse experience

Implement and publish this first as an **Unverified product example**. Promote it only after its
claim-local candidate, independent oracle, falsifiers, and acceptance requirements pass under
RFC 0089; evidence work must not block the earlier product path.

Status: future-state experience contract. It does not advance any structural
benchmark or claim shell support.

## Responsibility and public claim

The film shows the nonlinear load path of one explicitly imperfect,
axially compressed thin circular cylinder through mode initiation, peak load,
and a bounded post-buckling collapse interval. Geometry, imperfection,
constitutive law, end conditions, contact treatment, and load control remain
visible and versioned.

The public claim is deliberately narrower than “shell buckling is solved.”
NASA SP-8007 Rev 2 supplies design context and knockdown guidance, not an exact
oracle. Experimental validation is not claimed until one redistributable or
reproducibly acquired specimen dataset, including its measured imperfections,
is fixed by the scientific case.

The first film need not show fracture. It does not infer a universal knockdown
factor, general shell accuracy, or general contact/plasticity from one
specimen. If self-contact or plasticity is absent from the accepted case, it is
absent from the public claim and storyboard rather than simulated visually.

## Storyboard

| Presentation time | Content |
|---|---|
| 0--2 s | Radius, thickness, length, material, supports, axial load, and an honestly scaled imperfection preview |
| 2--11 s | Fixed camera with displacement magnitude or equivalent plastic strain as the sole primary field; load factor remains visible |
| 11--15 s | Load--end-shortening curve marks the current state, limit point, and accepted post-limit interval |
| 15--18 s | Labelled neutral cut back to the undeformed diagram; the collapse is never reversed |

Any deformation exaggeration is fixed and stated. The camera does not move to
hide a competing mode or contact event.

## Accepted-result evidence plan

The prerequisite ladder separates:

- large-rotation objectivity and finite-strain continuum verification;
- shell patch, membrane, bending, and locking cases;
- eigenvalue buckling against a classical solution;
- limit-point and snap-through continuation;
- contact and plasticity verification when either enters the film;
- imperfection-sensitive cylinder comparison and, separately, experimental
  validation when suitable data are fixed.

The decisive observable family is the load--end-shortening path, including
linear eigenvalue load, nonlinear peak load, post-limit branch, and the
normalised knockdown ratio. The case is rejected if the perfect-shell
refinement sequence fails its independently fixed classical limit or if the
pre-registered imperfection study does not show the expected sensitivity
direction. Agreement with a design knockdown band cannot compensate for
either failure.

Analytic limits and balance identities use dual independent derivation.
External tests and the NASA guidance retain their own uncertainty and
applicability rather than becoming derived expected values.

## Capability and artifact dependencies

- shell kinematics, sections, orientations, and locking control;
- geometric nonlinearity and an independently verified path-following method;
- imperfection identity and parameter-to-geometry lineage;
- plasticity and self-contact only if the retained case requires them;
- accepted load-step trajectories, shell-surface result projection, and
  synchronized curve playback.

The accepted scientific specification must select the specimen and its
constitutive/contact scope before implementation. This experience contract is
not permission to combine every nonlinear structural feature in one slice.

## Accessibility and promotion

The reduced-motion still pairs the critical deformation state with the full
load--shortening curve and marks the peak load. The text alternative names the
mode location and whether material yielding or contact has begun.

Promotion requires the complete prerequisite ladder applicable to the selected
case, accepted imperfection and load-path artifacts, a licensed external-data
plan for any validation claim, and common publication admission.
