# Full electric-motor multiphysics experience

Status: future-state experience contract. It does not advance electromagnetics,
rotating machinery, thermal, cooling-flow, or motor-system capability.

## Responsibility and public claim

The film shows one electric-motor system whose accepted electromagnetic,
circuit, rotational, thermal, and coolant-flow results exchange power through
explicit conserving interfaces. It presents torque production, loss
generation, temperature evolution, and heat removal one primary field at a
time.

No single famous motor benchmark verifies that entire composition. COMPUMAG
TEAM Problem 30 is the retained electromagnetic rotating-machine precursor,
not validation of the final cooled motor. Each subsystem has independent
verification or comparison evidence, and the assembled system claims only the
cross-domain balances and operating envelope actually demonstrated.

The film does not claim industrial motor fidelity, saturation, hysteresis,
three-dimensional end effects, manufacturing tolerances, general sliding
interfaces, general circuit/control libraries, conjugate heat transfer, or
experimental validation unless each is explicitly present in the accepted
case.

## Storyboard

| Presentation time | Content |
|---|---|
| 0--2 s | Motor section, phases/circuit, prescribed operating point, rotor, materials, thermal contacts, and coolant path |
| 2--6 s | Magnetic flux density is the sole primary field; rotor angle/speed and electromagnetic torque remain visible |
| 6--10 s | Accepted loss density replaces the field and transfers power into the thermal state |
| 10--14 s | Temperature replaces loss; a short coolant-flow projection shows the accepted heat-removal path |
| 14--18 s | Torque, electrical input, losses, stored/removed heat, and balance defect settle before a labelled neutral diagram reset |

Fields never overlap merely to suggest complexity. Rotor motion, field phase,
and thermal time are mapped explicitly because they occur on different
physical time scales.

## Evidence and falsifiers

The prerequisite ladder separates:

- magnetostatic and eddy-current analytic/manufactured verification;
- gauge, vector-space, interface, and torque/loss projection checks;
- TEAM Problem 30 torque, induced voltage, and loss comparisons under
  prescribed motion;
- circuit and rotational-network verification;
- heat generation, thermal conduction/contact, and coolant-flow verification;
- cross-domain power and energy balance for the retained assembled system.

The decisive system observable family is torque/speed, electrical input power,
electromagnetic and ohmic losses, stored thermal energy, coolant heat removal,
and the closed power/energy defect. The TEAM 30 precursor is rejected if any
retained spatial harmonic fails its source-derived slip, torque, voltage, or
loss relation. Fundamental synchronous speed does not imply zero total torque
when other winding harmonics remain. The final composition is rejected if its
cross-domain balance exceeds the precommitted budget, regardless of field
appearance.

Analytic limits and balance identities use dual independent oracles. TEAM
comparison values and any later experimental motor data are extracted and
normalised independently of the implementation.

## Capability and artifact dependencies

- low-frequency electromagnetic fields with an explicit gauge and conducting
  motion;
- prescribed and then coupled rotor motion, torque, voltage, current, and loss
  result projections;
- circuit/drive, thermal material/contact, and coolant-flow components;
- explicit power-transfer identities between every participating domain;
- multirate accepted trajectories and a film projection that never implies
  one shared physical time scale.

TEAM 30 is implemented and accepted before the final system is specified. The
full motor remains last in public delivery because it composes the largest
number of otherwise independent scientific seams.

## Accessibility and promotion

The reduced-motion alternative is an ordered four-panel still: magnetic field,
loss, temperature/coolant path, and power balance. The text alternative names
the transfer sequence and operating point; color is not the only indication
of hot or high-field regions.

Promotion requires accepted evidence for every displayed subsystem, the TEAM
30 precursor, explicit cross-domain balance, one accepted multirate result
lineage, and common publication admission. Experimental validation is a
separate promotion decision.
