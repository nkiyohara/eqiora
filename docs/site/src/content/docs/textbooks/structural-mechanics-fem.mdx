This intermediate series is for readers with statics, calculus, linear algebra,
and introductory continuum mechanics. It connects free-body reasoning,
kinematics, constitutive laws, and weak forms to interpretable finite-element
results.

  material capability.
</Aside>

## Learning outcomes

A reader should be able to state equilibrium, compatibility, kinematics,
constitutive assumptions, supports, and loads; derive weak and energy forms;
choose a bounded element and integration policy; interpret displacement,
strain, stress, reactions, and energy; and recognise locking, singularities,
instability, and mesh-dependent conclusions.

## Chapter map

1. **Forces, equilibrium, and energy** — sign conventions, reactions, virtual work, and units.
2. **Axial springs and bars** — exact displacement, reaction, and strain energy.
3. **Beam kinematics and boundaries** — cantilever, simply supported, and fixed–fixed behaviour.
4. **Continuum stress and strain** — small-strain tensors, traction, and constitutive assumptions.
5. **Weak forms and finite elements** — trial/test spaces, interpolation, quadrature, assembly, and constraints.
6. **Element completeness** — constant-strain patches, rigid motion, and orientation failures.
7. **Two-dimensional elasticity** — plane assumptions, mixed boundaries, reactions, and field observations.
8. **Stress concentration and singularity** — Kirsch and re-entrant-domain lessons and honest pointwise limits.
9. **Mesh distortion and locking** — bending-dominated and nearly incompressible response.
10. **Mass, damping, and vibration** — modes, transient response, resonance, and energy.
11. **Buckling and nonlinear paths** — stability, imperfections, large rotation, and path dependence.
12. **Contact and inequality constraints** — pressure, active sets, and mesh sensitivity.
13. **Verification of structural results** — patch, balance, energy, and refinement studies.
14. **Advanced structures** — bounded outlooks on shells, fracture, and collapse.

## Intended lesson spine

The initial sequence will use exact axial bars and beams, a constant-strain
patch, bounded two-dimensional continuum elasticity, a singular domain, and a
dynamic example. Every lesson separates deformation magnification and stress
recovery as presentation or derived choices.

## Publication boundary

The current [mixed-boundary elasticity Gallery page](/gallery/mixed-boundary-elasticity/)
presents one bounded verified path. It does not establish general materials,
nonlinear or finite-strain mechanics, arbitrary meshes, contact, dynamics, or
production solver behaviour. Lessons will consume reusable product paths rather
than introduce case-named assemblers or runners.
