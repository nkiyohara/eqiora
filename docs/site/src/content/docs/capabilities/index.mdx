This page answers **what can Eqiora do today, how was that conclusion reached,
and what remains outside the claim?** It is organized by problem family. The
repository <ExactSourceLink path="docs/capability-matrix.md" kind="blob">capability
matrix</ExactSourceLink> remains the maintained complete product map.

## Status vocabulary

These labels describe different facts; they are not a single maturity ladder.

- **Available** — a user-visible contract or surface exists. This alone says
  nothing about end-to-end execution or scientific correctness.
- **Executable** — at least one explicitly bounded path runs. A plausible result
  or image is not verification.
- **Checked** — focused product tests exercise the stated behavior, but no
  registered scientific claim is implied.
- **Verified** — reproducible registered evidence supports the exact stated
  claim. It does not make the surrounding product family mature or general.

<Aside type="caution" title="Read the boundary, not only the label">
  Every summary below is narrower than its section heading. Eqiora is alpha;
  none of these labels means production readiness, general geometry, every
  backend, or unrestricted dimensional support.
</Aside>

## Thermal

Eqiora does not currently claim a thermal component library or a supported
general heat-transfer problem class. A verified passive-scalar transport case
uses a field with temperature dimension, but the maintained matrix explicitly
does **not** promote that numerical case to a thermal-component, reaction, or
source-library claim. See the <ExactSourceLink path="docs/capability-matrix.md" kind="blob">thermal
and transport rows in the complete matrix</ExactSourceLink>.

## Technical catalog

The generated [evidence catalog](/evidence/) is the subordinate maintainer view.
It explains its Case, Status, Reference, Conformance kit, and Target columns,
keeps internal claim identifiers searchable and deep-linkable, and links to the
authoritative manifests. `/evidence/` remains the deliberate stable inbound
route; this page is the sole human Capabilities entrance.
